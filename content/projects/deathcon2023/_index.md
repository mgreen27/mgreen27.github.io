This workshop was presented at DEATHcon in November 2023. It combines an introduction to Velociraptor and VQL with practical investigation and detection scenarios. Follow the six labs in order, or choose a topic below. Five labs include links to the original video recordings.

## Before you start

The original labs use a Windows 10 or later virtual machine with administrator access, Velociraptor GUI mode and internet access for downloads and artifact imports. The GUI walkthrough used Velociraptor 0.7.0-3 at the time of writing. The UEFI lab also requires a UEFI-enabled VM.

The exercises include malware samples and system changes. Use a disposable lab VM and take a snapshot before starting, as described in the original material.
